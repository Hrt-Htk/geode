# What is Write the title last
The title is a promise about what the note says. Writing it first makes the note keep a promise
written before the writer knew what they meant.

# Why it matters
A note with an honest title can be found six months later by someone who has forgotten it exists.

# Where it showed up
Half the pages in [[Field notes book]] were renamed after their first read.
