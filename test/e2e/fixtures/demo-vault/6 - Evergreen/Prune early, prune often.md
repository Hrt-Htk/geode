# What is Prune early, prune often
Small cuts, made before the plant commits to a direction, cost nothing. The same cut made in July
costs a season.

# Why it matters
It works on troughs, on shelves and on drafts of a book: the earlier the trim, the less there is to
throw away.

# Where it showed up
The fig on the balcony grew into the window because nobody cut it in May.
